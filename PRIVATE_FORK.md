# Talvio project provenance

Talvio is the working company and product brand (it was Zynveliq until 2026-10-23; see docs/talvio-brand.md). It is an independently maintained derivative of SpeakoFlow by Abhishek Barali, itself derived from Handy by CJ Pais. The original MIT license and copyright notices remain in LICENSE.

Talvio began from a consolidated SpeakoFlow snapshot committed on 2026-10-22. On 2026-10-27, the complete public SpeakoFlow history through commit `967857fa5756e03e3bfa74d2ad003848063ba249` was joined to Talvio with a documented ancestry merge. Every restored source commit retains its original hash, parent graph, author, committer, message, and timestamps. Talvio's 190 pre-merge commits also remain unchanged.

## Repository relationship

Talvio and SpeakoFlow remain independent repositories; Talvio is not registered as a GitHub-native fork. The ancestry merge records the genuine code lineage and does not imply that the original authors endorse Talvio. The repository is public, so its source, history, and required license notices are publicly accessible.

Feedback submissions are blocked in the Rust backend. Update checks are disabled in the frontend, update endpoints and signing key are empty, update downloads are blocked in the backend, and automatic checks default to off. Upstream Windows signing credentials were removed. Inherited workflows are archived in .github/workflows-disabled and do not run.

Model downloads still contact their real hosting services. SpeakoFlow Mini remains a third-party model with its original name and download coordinates. Cloud providers, website links, and other external tools can make network requests when used. This is not a network anonymity or fully offline guarantee.

Configure real support and documentation links, update signing keys, endpoints, feedback service, and CI before enabling those services.
